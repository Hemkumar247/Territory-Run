Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024

1. Information We Collect

Territory Run collects the following data to provide the core game experience:

  • Location Data: GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, accuracy, altitude, speed) during active runs to track your trail and calculate territory.
  • Account Information: Email, display name, profile photo (via Google Sign-In or email/password), and a unique user ID.
  • Game Data: Run history, distance covered, territory claimed, achievements, streaks, and leaderboard position.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, app version, and push notification token (for territory alerts).
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous event data (run started, territory claimed, achievement unlocked) via Google Analytics 4.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Track and display your running trail in real-time
  • Calculate territory polygons and area claimed
  • Sync data across devices via Firebase
  • Show nearby territories and contested zones
  • Send push notifications for territory contests (opt-in)
  • Generate leaderboards and achievement progress
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only:

  • With Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, and cloud functions
  • With Google Analytics for anonymous usage statistics
  • With other players in your game session (display name, territory, distance - no precise location)
  • When required by law or to protect our rights

4. Location Data

  • Precise location is used while the app is open (to show you on the map) and during runs you explicitly start. During a run it keeps being collected when the screen is off or the app is in the background, with a visible notification, and stops when you tap Stop. We never collect location when no run is active and the app is closed.
  • Stored securely in Firebase Firestore
  • Used to calculate territory polygons (convex hull of your trail)
  • Shared with nearby players only as territory boundaries, not raw GPS points
  • You can delete all location data by deleting your account

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while account exists
  • Deleted accounts: All personal data purged within 30 days
  • Analytics data: Aggregated and anonymized after 14 months

6. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your data via the app's Delete Account feature
  • Disable location permissions in device settings (limits functionality)
  • Opt out of push notifications in app settings
  • Request data export by contacting support

7. Children's Privacy

Territory Run is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, contact us immediately.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (Firebase encryption). Authentication uses Firebase Auth with secure token management.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States or other countries where Google/Firebase operates. Adequate safeguards are in place per GDPR/CCPA.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted in the app with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
